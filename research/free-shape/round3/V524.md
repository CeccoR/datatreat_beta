# v524: the free shape without random numbers, on round 3's screening runs

v524 takes the last random numbers out of the Rietveld engine. To v523 the free shape's starts were tilted by
Gaussian noise from a seeded stream (0.08 rad on each component of their axes), the stream skipped the deviates
v522's random starts had drawn, and the setting `shapeStarts` above its default added random starts from a second
stream. Now each start is tilted 0.1 rad (5.7°, the mean of the drawn tilt) toward the part across its axis of one
fixed Cartesian direction, the axis across it turned as much about it (`SHAPE_TILT`), and the setting is gone. The
result no longer depends on any stream; it does depend on the tilt, as it did on the seed: round 2 (`STEP4.md`)
saw the seed move a clean row by 7.5 χ²_ν.

The question is whether the statistics of the frozen search hold. Run on the 77 screening runs of round 3's tuning
part (66 truths, 11 spheres; no validation row), nice 15, three processes, against the frozen prototype re-run on
the same rows, and against the same prototype with its tilts drawn from two other seeds: the spread a draw of the
tilt makes. All data synthetic; the records are in the work folder (`$W/fs2/r3/results/`), not here.

## Reading

- **v524 lies within the spread of v523's own tilt draws** on every statistic: outcomes, the keep test, reach
  (60 against 60–62), accuracy (the clean worst extent's median +3 % against +2 %, its 90 % point Z2's), the
  intervals' coverage (clean extents 76/82 as Z1's, clean axes 31/32 as all), the check, and the evaluations
  (1.02×, inside 1.01–1.06×).
- **Its misses are the tilt's, not new.** box-Pm-3-near-a, which round 2's search missed by 1145 χ²_ν and v523's
  line starts reach, is reached from a line start only under some tilts: Z2 misses it by 47.5, N by 1292.5 (the
  per-line check then flags the clean row, z 6.3, the misfit being the search's). ellcyl-P4mmm-generic-a is missed
  alike by Z1, N and M (148.1); sqbox-P2m-near-a (texture) by every tag but Z, by 771 to 1418.
- **Variant M** (a different direction for each start) is no better: one row fewer reached, one axis fewer covered.
  The fixed direction stays: the simplest, as asked. Neither changes the frozen tunables.

Shortfall: 77 runs show the spread of a few draws, not a rate; a clean truth reachable only from some tilts
(box-Pm-3-near-a) is a completeness failure of the search, as round 3's four clean tuning misses were
(`CONFIRM.md` §2).
