Groups and scale

The images are grouped by pixel size (within 10 % of each other), each group named by its magnification; a group's images share one scale bar in the figure. At common scale a group's images are brought to its largest pixel size (the others reduced by averaging, never enlarged) and to its smallest field of view, each crop narrowed about its middle (dashed in the crop editor). At own scale each image keeps its pixels and its crop, and gets its own bar. Click a group's name to rename it. A row of images wider than the box turns a page at a time with the arrows at its ends. Drag an image onto another group to move it there, or onto + Group to start a new one with it; + Group clicked adds an empty group, to drag images into. Reset makes the groups again from the pixel sizes. Each group makes its own figure: Figure composer lays its images out on a page in millimetres, as the plots' composer does, with their names, letters if wanted and the scale bars, for PNG at a chosen dpi or SVG.

Crop and intensity

Images. Every image, with its crop; a click shows it in the editor. The chain on a thumbnail links its image: linked images share one crop, narrowed to fit wherever an image is smaller (unlinked, an image keeps the crop it had). Link all links the images chosen in the selector, around the crop of the one in the editor. The selector chooses which images the intensity settings apply to: All, every image alike; a group, that group's images alone, the others keeping theirs. Going from All to the groups, each group starts from All's settings; going back to All, All takes the settings of the group on show. Crop. Each image starts with its default crop, the whole image less its info bar: Default ticked, its fields are locked; drag the frame's handles or sides to resize it, inside it to move it (which unticks Default). A ratio, when locked, holds while resizing. The toolbar on the image fits it, pans it, or zooms into an area (with a tool on, the wheel zooms and a double click fits). Intensity. Each image's grey levels go through one curve, drawn over the histogram of its crop: first the normalisation (none; a stretch of each image between two percentiles of its own levels; or the reference image's mean and SD, or its whole histogram, given to every image), then contrast, brightness and gamma; This image adds its own correction on top. Each Default locks its settings at their defaults. Statistics come from each crop, its saturated pixels left out. The images themselves are never changed.
